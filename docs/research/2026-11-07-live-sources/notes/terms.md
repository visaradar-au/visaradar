# Terms, limits and risks (sub-agent notes, 2026-11-07)

- ABS: CC BY 4.0 International (https://www.abs.gov.au/website-privacy-copyright-and-disclaimer, release 13/04/2026). Excludes microdata, logos, Coat of Arms. No automated-access clause.
- Federal Register of Legislation: CC BY 4.0 International (https://www.legislation.gov.au/terms-of-use). Required wording: "Sourced from the Federal Register of Legislation at [full date of download]. For the latest information on Australian Government law please go to https://www.legislation.gov.au." No scraping clause; reuse page asks for crawl delays and off-peak crawls.
- Home Affairs: CC BY 4.0 per an AMEP subdomain snippet (main copyright pages 403). Excludes Coat of Arms, logo, third-party content. Medium confidence.
- JSA publications CC BY 4.0; some data.gov.au JSA datasets CC BY 2.5 AU. Check per dataset.
- SkillSelect EOI dashboard: no specific terms found; FOI replies say the department reviewed it to prevent re-identification. Aggregated republication with attribution is low copyright risk. Fragile access; no documented permission.
- State migration sites: not verified directly (earlier research: QLD and WA forbid reuse of content; SA allows reproduction without alteration).
- s276/s280: immigration assistance = using migration knowledge to assist a visa applicant with their application. General statistics and official information for everyone, with no personalised advice, appears outside it (inference; no OMARA guidance found).
